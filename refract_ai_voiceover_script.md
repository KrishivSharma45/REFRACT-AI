# REFRACT AI — Demo Voiceover Script

**Target runtime:** ~3:45–4:00
**Pace:** ~140 words/minute (a touch slower than normal conversation — internship reviewers are listening for clarity, not speed)
**Delivery note:** Read it like you're walking a smart friend through something you're proud of, not reciting a pitch. Let the small pauses (—) actually land as pauses.

Record the screen first (or record live), then read each block roughly when that part of the app is on screen. The timestamps are a guide, not a metronome — reading naturally matters more than hitting them exactly.

---

### [0:00–0:15] — Intro
**On screen:** REFRACT landing page, sitting on the hero section before you click anything.

> "Hi, I'm Krishiv Sharma. For this project I wanted to solve something almost every job seeker has felt — getting rejected from an application and never actually finding out why. So I built REFRACT: an app that turns your rejection history into something you can actually learn from."

---

### [0:15–0:40] — Landing page / the pitch
**On screen:** Scroll slowly from the hero down through the "Product" section.

> "The core idea is simple: rejection has signals, they're just usually invisible. You give REFRACT your resume and a target job description, and instead of a vague score, it gives you evidence-backed signals — explainable, traceable reasoning you can actually act on, not a black box."

---

### [0:25–0:55] — Uploading documents
**On screen:** Click "Analyze an application," show the three upload cards (resume, job description, rejection/feedback).

> "You start by giving it evidence. Your resume, the job description, and — if you have one — a rejection email or feedback note. Everything is processed locally, right inside your session, so nothing about your resume gets shared or sold anywhere."

---

### [0:55–1:35] — Overall signal & evidence strength
**On screen:** Results page, Section 01 (Overall signal) then Section 02 (Evidence strength table).

> "Once it runs, REFRACT gives you a primary signal — the strongest thing your resume actually proves — along with a confidence score and an overall alignment percentage. Then it breaks that down requirement by requirement: what's strongly demonstrated, what's weak, and what's missing entirely. So instead of guessing why you didn't hear back, you can see exactly where the gap is."

---

### [1:35–2:10] — Priority engine & role direction
**On screen:** Section 03 (Priority engine) then Section 04 (Role direction).

> "It doesn't just list problems, it ranks them. The priority engine tells you which gaps matter most — high, medium, or low — so you know what to fix first. And the role direction section shows you where your evidence currently points. Sometimes that confirms the role you applied for. Sometimes it reveals a better-fitting direction you hadn't considered yet."

---

### [2:10–2:35] — Personal roadmap
**On screen:** Section 05 (Personal roadmap).

> "All of that comes together into a personal roadmap — a short, ordered set of next steps. Close this gap first, build that evidence next, align toward this direction. Improvement stops being a mystery and starts being a plan."

---

### [2:35–3:15] — Insights, history & patterns
**On screen:** Navigate to the Insights page — KPIs, the evidence trajectory graph, the comparison tool, and the recurring patterns cards.

> "And because job searching isn't a one-time event, REFRACT remembers. Every analysis gets saved to your history, so you can watch your evidence trajectory improve over time, compare any two applications side by side, and — this is my favorite part — surface recurring patterns. If the same gap keeps showing up across five different applications, that's not bad luck. That's a signal worth listening to."

---

### [3:15–3:35] — Voice control (nice-to-have touch)
**On screen:** Click the mic button, speak a command like "open insights."

> "I also added voice control, so you can just say something like 'start analysis' or 'open insights' and REFRACT responds. It's a small touch, but it makes the app feel less like a form you fill out and more like a tool that's actually working alongside you."

---

### [3:35–3:55] — Closing
**On screen:** Return to the landing page, or hold on a clean final shot.

> "REFRACT was my attempt to turn one of the most frustrating parts of job hunting — silence — into something useful: evidence, direction, and a plan. Thank you for watching, and I'd genuinely love the chance to bring this same way of thinking to your team."

---

## Quick tips for recording

- **Breathe before each section break.** The dashes between blocks are natural places to pause and let a new screen settle before you start talking over it.
- **Cut the intro personal line if you're reusing this for a portfolio reel** — everything from "The core idea is simple" onward stands on its own.
- **If you're going over 4 minutes**, the safest trims are the voice-control section (3:15–3:35) and shortening the closing line — the analysis walkthrough (0:55–2:35) is the part reviewers care about most.
- **If you're under time**, you can slow down rather than add — a slightly slower, more confident pace reads better on video than rushed extra content.
